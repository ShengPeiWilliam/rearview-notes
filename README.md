# Rearview Notes

[Rearview](https://rearview-driver.vercel.app) shows a delivery driver their own numbers. These notes are the next step: I take one of those numbers, ask what I believed about it from behind the wheel, and check whether the data actually backs me up.

The part I care about is the reasoning, not the chart. Each post is one question with one idea to test it, worked out on my own two months of data, and honest about what so little can and can't settle.

| # | The hunch | What my data said | The idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weekends are easier, late nights are slower. ([read](https://shengpeiwilliam.github.io/rearview-notes/posts/01-does-my-experience-hold-up/)) | Neither holds up. Two months can't tell weekends from weekdays, or late nights from earlier. | Bootstrap, and what to resample |
| 2 | Saturdays are slow. ([read](https://shengpeiwilliam.github.io/rearview-notes/posts/02-was-i-driving-somewhere-slow/)) | Only at lunch. Split by time of day, the slow Saturday is a lunch-hour effect; dinner looks like any other day. | Confounding, and holding the hour steady |
